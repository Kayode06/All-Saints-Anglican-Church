Societies & Organisations

All Saints' is home to a wide network of societies and organisations, each serving a different age group and calling within the church family. Tap a group below to learn more about what it does and how to get involved.

  • Men's Auxiliary Association

    A fellowship for the men of the parish.

  • Young Men Christian Association

    Fellowship and discipleship for young men.

  • Girls' Guild

    A guild for girls in the parish.

  • Mothers' Union

    Support and fellowship for mothers and married women.

  • Women's Guild

    A guild serving the women of the church.

  • Egbe Ife Olorun

    A society devoted to the love of God.

  • Argonauts Society of Nigeria

    A Christian society within the parish.

  • Anglican Youth Fellowship (Nigeria)

    Fellowship and formation for parish youth.

  • The Boys' Scout Movement

    Scouting and character development for boys.

  • Ladies League Society

    A league for the women of All Saints'.

  • Young Women Christian Fellowship

    Fellowship for young women in the parish.

  • Morning Star Society

    A parish society open to members.

  • The Band of Hope Society

    A long-standing society of the parish.

  • The Goodwill Society

    A society built on goodwill and service.

  • The Boys' Brigade Nigeria

    Christian discipline and mentorship for boys.

  • The Church Medical Society

    Health support and outreach within the parish.

  • Busy Bees Society

    An active parish service society.

  • Parish Youth Council

    The umbrella council for youth activities.

  • Men's Christian Circle (MCC)

    A Christian circle for the men of the church.

  • Ladies Christian Circle

    A Christian circle for the women of the church.

  • The Progressive Youth Association

    An association for the parish's young people.

  • Torch Bearers Society

    A society carrying the light of faith forward.

  • The Fountain of Faith Society

    A society rooted in faith and renewal.

  • Youth Christian Circle (YCC)

    Fellowship for youth in the parish.

  • The Youth Christian Band

    A musical band serving parish worship.

  • The Girls' Brigade Nigeria

    Christian discipline and mentorship for girls.

  • Ladies' Progressive Society

    A progressive society for the women of the parish.

  • Anglican Youth Fellowship Stars (AYF Stars)

    A youth fellowship group for younger members.
