The Service Units

The Service Units are the ministries that keep worship and parish life running week to week — from the choir stalls to the church library. Each unit welcomes new hands.

  • The Choir

    Leads the parish in sung worship at every service.

  • Guild of Stewards

    Welcomes worshippers and keeps services running smoothly.

  • Altar Servers' Guild (Acolytes)

    Assists the clergy at the altar during services.

  • Readers (formerly "Lay Readers")

    Reads Scripture and leads portions of the liturgy.

  • The Church Library

    Provides books and resources for spiritual growth.

  • The ICT Department

    Handles sound, streaming, and digital media for the parish.
