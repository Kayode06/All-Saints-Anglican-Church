Our Story

A Brief History

Founded in Faith, Growing in Grace

All Saints' Anglican Church Yaba has stood as a beacon of faith in the heart of Yaba for generations. Established in the rich Anglican tradition, our church has been a spiritual home to countless families, a place where the Gospel is proclaimed faithfully and lives are transformed by Christ's love.

Throughout our history, we have remained steadfast in our commitment to preaching the Word of God, celebrating the sacraments, and serving our community with compassion and integrity. Our parish has grown from a small gathering to a vibrant congregation that reflects the diversity and warmth of Lagos.

We believe that every person is created in the image of God, and we exist to draw people into a living, transformative relationship with Jesus Christ. Rooted in the liturgical traditions of the Anglican Communion and guided by Scripture, we blend reverent worship with Spirit-filled ministry.
