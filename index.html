Who We Are

Our Story

All Saints' Anglican Church Yaba is a vibrant, welcoming community rooted in the traditions of the Anglican Communion and guided by the Word of God. For generations, we have been a lighthouse of faith in the heart of Yaba, Lagos.

We believe every person is created in the image of God, and that the Church exists to draw people into a living relationship with Jesus Christ. Our services blend reverent liturgy with Spirit-filled worship. Read More
